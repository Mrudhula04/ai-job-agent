## ⚠️ Important Notes

**For STEM OPT Extension:**
- ✅ Company MUST be E-Verify enrolled
- ✅ Job MUST be related to your degree
- ✅ Apply 90 days before OPT expires

**Red Flags (Avoid):**
- ❌ "US Citizen only" in job description
- ❌ "No visa sponsorship"
- ❌ Very small startups (<10 employees)
- ❌ Companies with no H-1B history

## 🎉 Success Tips

1. **Apply Early** - Don't wait until last minute
2. **Apply Broadly** - 100+ applications is normal
3. **Network** - LinkedIn, referrals help a lot
4. **Follow Up** - Email recruiters after applying
5. **Be Flexible** - Consider contract roles, different locations
